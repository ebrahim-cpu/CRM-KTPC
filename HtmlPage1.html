About Malaysia

Malaysia is a country located in Southeast Asia, made up of two regions: Peninsular Malaysia and East Malaysia. The capital city is Kuala Lumpur, and the official language is Malay. Malaysia has a diverse population, with many different ethnic groups such as Malays, Chinese, and Indians. The country is known for its delicious food, beautiful beaches, and diverse culture.
